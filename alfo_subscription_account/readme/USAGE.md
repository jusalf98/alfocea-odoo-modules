1. Open a subscription and check that its **Renewal Document** is *Invoice*.
2. In its plan, choose whether the renewal invoices are posted automatically (**Post Renewal Invoices**).
3. Every day, the scheduled action *Subscriptions: create renewal invoices* creates the invoices or vendor bills of the subscriptions whose next invoice date is today or earlier. To test it without waiting, set the **Next Invoice Date** of an active subscription to today and run the action from *Settings > Technical > Scheduled Actions*.
4. Use the **Invoices** smart button of the subscription, or the **Subscription** smart button of the invoice, to go from one to the other.
