Invoices the Alfocea subscriptions directly, without sale or purchase
orders.

A daily scheduled action creates, for every subscription renewed with
**Invoice** whose next invoice date has been reached:

- A **customer invoice** for customer subscriptions, or a **vendor
  bill** for vendor subscriptions.
- One document per period, dated on the first day of the period, with
  the subscription lines and prices and the period dates in the line
  labels.
- Missed periods are caught up, and no period starting after the
  subscription end date is invoiced.
- Each plan decides whether its renewal invoices are posted
  automatically or left as drafts (**Post Renewal Invoices**, disabled
  by default).

**No double renewal.** Every subscription has a **Renewal Document**
(core module), and each renewal scheduled action only processes its own
document. Invoices are the default only when no other integration
applies: with *Subscription Sale* installed, customer subscriptions
default to sale orders; with *Subscription Purchase* installed, vendor
subscriptions default to purchase orders. The document can be changed on
each subscription.

Smart buttons link the subscriptions and their invoices in both
directions.

Usage
=====

1. Open a subscription and check that its **Renewal Document** is
   *Invoice*.
2. In its plan, choose whether the renewal invoices are posted
   automatically (**Post Renewal Invoices**).
3. Every day, the scheduled action *Subscriptions: create renewal
   invoices* creates the invoices or vendor bills of the subscriptions
   whose next invoice date is today or earlier. To test it without
   waiting, set the **Next Invoice Date** of an active subscription to
   today and run the action from *Settings > Technical > Scheduled
   Actions*.
4. Use the **Invoices** smart button of the subscription, or the
   **Subscription** smart button of the invoice, to go from one to the
   other.
