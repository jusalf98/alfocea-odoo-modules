Connects the Alfocea subscriptions with the sale orders, in both
directions.

**From sale orders to subscriptions.** When a quotation is confirmed,
every line whose product has a **Subscription Plan** creates and starts
a customer subscription for the order customer:

- One subscription per plan, with the subscription lines of the order
  (product, description, quantity and discounted price in the company
  currency).
- If the customer already has an open subscription (draft, trial, active
  or paused) with that plan **and that product**, the quantity is added
  to it.
- A product the customer does not have yet creates a new subscription,
  unless the plan has **One Subscription per Partner** enabled: then it
  is added as a new line to the existing one.
- The order is only linked to the subscriptions that contain its
  products.
- Cancelling the order reverts what it changed in the subscriptions.
- When confirming from the form an order that changes existing
  subscriptions, a wizard shows what they already have and what the
  order adds, with three options: **Confirm and Add**, **Confirm without
  Adding** (the existing subscriptions are not changed; new ones are
  still created) and **Cancel**.
- The confirmed order covers the first period, so the first renewal is
  due one period later.
- **Free trial:** the quotation lines in trial are shown in green. A
  quotation line whose product starts a new subscription with a trial
  plan gets a 100% discount and a note in its description ("Free trial:
  7 days. Then billed every 1 Month(s)."). The customer pays nothing
  now: the subscription keeps the real price and its first invoice is at
  the end of the trial. Buying the product again, or adding it to an
  existing subscription, is never a trial. Only quotations are flagged:
  confirmed orders keep their discounts.
- The subscriptions are created as superuser, so salesmen don't need any
  subscription access right to confirm orders.

**From subscriptions to sale orders.** A daily scheduled action creates
the renewal sale orders of the customer subscriptions whose next invoice
date has been reached:

- One sale order per period, with the subscription lines and prices, and
  the period dates in the line descriptions (in the customer language).
- Missed periods are caught up, and no period starting after the
  subscription end date is ordered.
- The renewal orders keep the salesperson and sales team of the order
  that created the subscription.
- Each plan decides whether its renewal orders are confirmed
  automatically or left as quotations (**Confirm Renewal Orders**).
- An error in one subscription is logged and does not block the renewal
  of the others.

Smart buttons link the sale orders and their subscriptions in both
directions.

Usage
=====

1. As a subscription administrator, open a product and set its
   **Subscription Plan** (next to the product category).
2. In the plan, choose whether the renewal orders are confirmed
   automatically (**Confirm Renewal Orders**, enabled by default).
3. Create a quotation with that product and confirm it. The subscription
   is created and started automatically. Use the **Subscriptions** smart
   button of the order to open it.
4. Every day, the scheduled action *Subscriptions: create renewal sale
   orders* creates the sale orders of the subscriptions whose next
   invoice date is today or earlier, and moves that date to the next
   period. To test it without waiting, set the **Next Invoice Date** of
   an active subscription to today and run the action from *Settings >
   Technical > Scheduled Actions*.
5. Use the **Sale Orders** smart button of the subscription to see all
   its orders.
