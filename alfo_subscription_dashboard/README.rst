Analysis of the Alfocea subscriptions, in *Subscriptions > Reporting*:

- **Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: the monthly recurring revenue of the
  active customer subscriptions, by plan (graph and pivot, with the
  ARR).
- **New Subscriptions**: subscriptions started per month.
- **Churn**: cancelled and expired customer subscriptions per month and
  close reason, with the MRR lost.
- **Recurring Costs**: the monthly recurring cost of the vendor
  subscriptions, by vendor.

Every subscription gets two stored amounts, so they can be grouped and
filtered like any other field:

- **MRR**: the total per period normalized to one month (a yearly plan
  of 1,200 counts 100 per month; a weekly plan of 10 counts about 43 per
  month).
- **ARR**: MRR x 12.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Subscriptions > Reporting* and open one of the analyses.
2. Switch between the graph, the pivot and the list, and group by plan,
   customer, month or close reason.
3. To analyse churn reasons, cancel the subscriptions with a **Close
   Reason** (the Cancel button asks for it).
