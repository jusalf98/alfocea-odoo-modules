Analysis of the Alfocea subscriptions, in *Subscriptions > Reporting*:

- **Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: the monthly recurring revenue of the active customer subscriptions, by plan (graph and pivot, with the ARR).
- **New Subscriptions**: subscriptions started per month.
- **Churn**: cancelled and expired customer subscriptions per month and close reason, with the MRR lost.
- **Recurring Costs**: the monthly recurring cost of the vendor subscriptions, by vendor.

Every subscription gets two stored amounts, so they can be grouped and filtered like any other field:

- **MRR**: the total per period normalized to one month (a yearly plan of 1,200 counts 100 per month; a weekly plan of 10 counts about 43 per month).
- **ARR**: MRR x 12.
