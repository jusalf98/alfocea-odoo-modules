Connects the Alfocea vendor subscriptions with the purchase orders, in
both directions.

**From purchase orders to subscriptions.** When a purchase order is
confirmed (directly or after its approval), every line whose product has
a **Vendor Subscription Plan** creates and starts a vendor subscription
for the order vendor:

- One subscription per plan, with the subscription lines of the order
  (product, description, quantity and discounted price in the company
  currency).
- If the vendor already has an open subscription (draft, trial, active
  or paused) with that plan **and that product**, the quantity is added
  to it.
- A product the vendor does not have yet creates a new subscription,
  unless the plan has **One Subscription per Partner** enabled: then it
  is added as a new line to the existing one.
- The order is only linked to the subscriptions that contain its
  products.
- Cancelling the order reverts what it changed in the subscriptions.
- When confirming from the form an order that changes existing
  subscriptions, a wizard shows what they already have and what the
  order adds, with three options: **Confirm and Add**, **Confirm without
  Adding** (the existing subscriptions are not changed; new ones are
  still created) and **Cancel**.
- The confirmed order covers the first period, so the first renewal is
  due one period later. With a trial plan, the order is paid to the
  vendor, so it covers the first period after the trial.
- The vendor subscription plan is a different field from the (customer)
  subscription plan of the core module: a product can be sold with a
  plan and purchased without one, and the other way around.
- The subscriptions are created as superuser, so buyers don't need any
  subscription access right.

**From subscriptions to purchase orders.** A daily scheduled action
creates the renewal purchase orders of the vendor subscriptions renewed
with purchase orders, when their next invoice date has been reached:

- One purchase order per period, with the subscription lines and prices,
  and the period dates in the line descriptions.
- Missed periods are caught up, and no period starting after the
  subscription end date is ordered.
- The renewal orders keep the buyer of the order that created the
  subscription.
- Each plan decides whether its renewal purchase orders are confirmed
  automatically or left as requests for quotation (**Confirm Renewal
  Purchase Orders**).

Smart buttons link the purchase orders and their subscriptions in both
directions.

Usage
=====

1. As a subscription administrator, open a product and set its **Vendor
   Subscription Plan** (below the subscription plan, next to the product
   category).
2. Confirm a purchase order with that product. The vendor subscription
   (``PSUB/`` reference) is created and started automatically. Use the
   **Subscriptions** smart button of the order to open it.
3. Every day, the scheduled action *Subscriptions: create renewal
   purchase orders* creates the purchase orders of the vendor
   subscriptions whose next invoice date is today or earlier. To test it
   without waiting, set the **Next Invoice Date** of an active vendor
   subscription to today and run the action from *Settings > Technical >
   Scheduled Actions*.
4. Use the **Purchase Orders** smart button of the subscription to see
   all its orders.
