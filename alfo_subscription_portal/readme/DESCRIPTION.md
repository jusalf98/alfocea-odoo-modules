Customer portal for the Alfocea subscriptions.

In *My Account > Subscriptions*, the customers (and their contacts) see their customer subscriptions:

- **List** with the plan, the billing periodicity, the next invoice, the total per period and the status.
- **Detail** with the dates (start, next invoice, end of the free trial, end date), the lines, the total per period and the communication history.
- **PDF download** of the subscription.
- **Self-service actions**, if their plan allows them (*Customers Can Pause* and *Customers Can Cancel* options of the plan):
  - **Pause** and **Resume**.
  - **Cancel** the subscription, choosing a close reason (required) and an optional comment, so that the cancellation appears in the churn report. For plans renewed automatically, **Do not renew** it instead (only before the notice deadline).

Every action of the customer is recorded in the chatter of the subscription, with the customer as author. Record rules make sure that a portal user only sees the customer subscriptions of their own company. The subscription can also be shared with a link (access token), like other Odoo documents.
