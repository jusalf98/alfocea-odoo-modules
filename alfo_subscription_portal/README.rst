Customer portal for the Alfocea subscriptions.

In *My Account > Subscriptions*, the customers (and their contacts) see
their customer subscriptions:

- **List** with the plan, the billing periodicity, the next invoice, the
  total per period and the status.
- **Detail** with the dates (start, next invoice, end of the free trial,
  end date), the lines, the total per period and the communication
  history.
- **PDF download** of the subscription.
- **Self-service actions**, if their plan allows them (*Customers Can
  Pause* and *Customers Can Cancel* options of the plan):

  - **Pause** and **Resume**.
  - **Cancel** the subscription, or for plans renewed automatically,
    **Do not renew** it (only before the notice deadline).

Every action of the customer is recorded in the chatter of the
subscription, with the customer as author. Record rules make sure that a
portal user only sees the customer subscriptions of their own company.
The subscription can also be shared with a link (access token), like
other Odoo documents.

Usage
=====

1. Give portal access to the customer (*Contacts > Action > Grant portal
   access*).
2. In the plan, enable **Customers Can Pause** and/or **Customers Can
   Cancel** if the customers may do it themselves.
3. The customer logs in and opens *My Account > Subscriptions*.
