1. Give your users the *Subscriptions / User* or *Subscriptions / Administrator* access right.
2. Go to *Subscriptions > Configuration > Plans* and create a plan.
3. To sell a subscription, go to *Subscriptions > Customer Subscriptions*, create a subscription, choose the customer and the plan, and add the product lines.
4. To register a subscription you pay for, go to *Subscriptions > Vendor Subscriptions*, choose the vendor and the plan, and add the product lines. Only purchasable products are proposed, and their price comes from the vendor pricelist or the product cost.
5. Click **Start**. The subscription goes to *Trial* if the plan has trial days, or to *Active* otherwise.
6. Use **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel** and **Reset to Draft** to manage its lifecycle.
