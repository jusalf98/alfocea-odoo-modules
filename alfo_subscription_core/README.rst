Core module of the Alfocea subscription suite. It manages both the
subscriptions you sell to your customers and the ones you contract with
your vendors. It adds:

- **Subscription plans**: billing periodicity (days, weeks, months or
  years), interval and free trial days. Plans are shared by both kinds
  of subscriptions.
- **Subscription contracts** of two types:

  - **Customer subscriptions** (``SUB/`` reference): the line prices
    come from the product sales price.
  - **Vendor subscriptions** (``PSUB/`` reference): the line prices come
    from the vendor pricelist of the product, or from its cost if the
    vendor has no price.

- Each contract has a partner, a plan, start and end dates, a next
  invoice date, product lines and lifecycle states (draft, trial,
  active, paused, cancelled and expired).
- A daily scheduled action that moves finished trials to active and
  finished contracts to expired.
- Two security groups: *Subscriptions / User* and *Subscriptions /
  Administrator*.

Invoicing, sales and portal features are provided by other modules of
the suite.

Usage
=====

1. Give your users the *Subscriptions / User* or *Subscriptions /
   Administrator* access right.
2. Go to *Subscriptions > Configuration > Plans* and create a plan.
3. To sell a subscription, go to *Subscriptions > Customer
   Subscriptions*, create a subscription, choose the customer and the
   plan, and add the product lines.
4. To register a subscription you pay for, go to *Subscriptions > Vendor
   Subscriptions*, choose the vendor and the plan, and add the product
   lines. Only purchasable products are proposed, and their price comes
   from the vendor pricelist or the product cost.
5. Click **Start**. The subscription goes to *Trial* if the plan has
   trial days, or to *Active* otherwise.
6. Use **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel** and **Reset to Draft** to
   manage its lifecycle.
